# Alchemical Artisans

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/web-alchemicalartisans.com-bf9431)](https://www.alchemicalartisans.com)
[![License: CC BY-NC 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/content-CC%20BY--NC%204.0-67341c)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

Technology consulting practised as a craft. We are a two-person practice that
takes tangled systems, the legacy code, the stalled delivery, the brittle
infrastructure, and works them step by patient step into something sound.

*Solve et Coagula.*

## What we do

We take on architecture reviews, modernisation of legacy systems, cloud and
platform work, data engineering, and accessibility and inclusive design.

- **Systems architecture and review.** An outside read on how a system is put together and where it is likely to bend or break.
- **Legacy modernisation.** Moving old code and data forward without a risky big-bang rewrite.
- **Cloud and platform work.** The infrastructure and pipelines that let a team ship.
- **Data engineering.** Getting data to move reliably and mean something once it lands.
- **Accessibility and inclusive design.** Building so the result works for the widest range of people, devices, and situations.

## How we work

Every engagement starts with one question: what does success look like, and how
will we know we got there. We spend real time learning the context, then we say
what we found, including the parts that are awkward to hear. Sometimes the answer
is a large project. Often it is a handful of small changes that unblock the team
and let them carry on without us.

We keep a public working journal of what we learn at
[alchemicalartisans.com](https://www.alchemicalartisans.com).
