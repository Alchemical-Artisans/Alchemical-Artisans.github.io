description of good consulting work too.

### A quick word on what tech consulting actually is

Tech consulting gets talked about as if it were one thing. In practice it covers
a wide range, from a few days of a second opinion to a multi month rebuild. What
the useful versions have in common is fairly simple.

You bring in someone from outside because you want a clear view of a problem and
a credible plan for fixing it. That person spends time understanding your
system, your constraints, and the outcome you care about. They tell you what
they found, including the parts you did not want to hear. Then they either hand
you a plan you can run yourself, or they stay and help you run it.

Good consulting is not about arriving with a template and forcing your situation
into it. It is about doing the unglamorous work of learning your context, then
being honest about the trade offs. Sometimes the right answer is a large
project. Often it is a handful of small changes that unblock the team and let
them carry on without us.
