Recently I've been interested in our local government, specifically
how it operates and the people involved. The decisions at the state
and federal level get more attention, but the real work happens closer
to home.

Not only are those decisions impactful, they're also the ones where
you can have the most impact. The meetings where those decisions are
made are open to the public, and if you show up your voice can carry a
lot of weight.

The challenge is that information on these meetings can be difficult
to track down. My state's [open meeting laws](https://www.mass.gov/the-open-meeting-law)
ensure that [agendas and schedules](https://www.haverhillma.gov/government/agendas-and-minutes/)
are available, but the information is disorganized and the documents
difficult to navigate.

As a software enginer, this is an area where I can help, and so I'm
introducing [Meetinghouse](https://haverhill.alchemicalartisans.com/).
Any time an agenda or minutes are published for one of Haverhill's
local meetings, it will show up on an easy to read calendar. Following
the link will provide information on how to attend and break down the
information available so it's easy to find relevant details.

My hope is to create something that makes the process easier for
everyone involved. Please check it out, and if you have feedback feel
free to [email me](mailto:cto@alchemicalartisans.com), I'd love to hear from
you.
