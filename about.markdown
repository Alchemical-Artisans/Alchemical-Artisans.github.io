Alchemical Artisans is a two-person technology consulting practice. Between us we
cover architecture reviews, modernisation of legacy systems, cloud and platform
work, data engineering, and accessibility.

The idea is simple. You bring in an outside pair of eyes to get a clear read on a
problem and a credible plan for fixing it. We spend time learning your system,
your constraints, and the outcome you care about, then we tell you what we found
and what we would do about it. Sometimes that is a large project. Often it is a
handful of small changes that unblock the team.

We keep a working journal of what we learn. If you want a fuller sense of how we
think, start with [What We Do]({% post_url 2026-09-01-what-this-workshop-is-for %}),
or email [contact@alchemicalartisans.com](mailto:contact@alchemicalartisans.com).
